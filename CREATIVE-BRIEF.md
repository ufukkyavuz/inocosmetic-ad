# Catch — Creative Strategy & Production Plan

## Content Pillars

| Pillar | Application for Catch |
|---|---|
| **Pain Point** | "Routine takes too long," "can't find shades that match," "sunscreen that doesn't feel like sunscreen" |
| **Social Proof** | Reviews/ratings on Sets, before/after for Catch Glow, UGC unboxing of bundle Sets |
| **Product Demo** | Application/swatch videos for Bloom lip & cheek, texture close-ups, shade range pan |
| **Offer** | Set bundle savings vs. buying SKUs individually (price anchoring — you already have this built into the Sets) |
| **Education** | Ingredient/benefit explainers for Collagen Shot and sunscreen — **claims-reviewed copy only** (see compliance note in `ADS-STRATEGY.md`) |

## What Works for This Category

- **UGC unboxing/routine videos**: outperform studio-only content (Spark/Reels-style ~3% CTR vs ~2% for standard static)
- **Before/after**: strong fit for Catch Glow and skin-benefit claims (stay within approved claims language)
- **Shade-range / "find your match" content**: reduces the #1 DTC beauty objection (wrong shade) before purchase
- **Set bundling as the hero, not an upsell**: lead with "Ultimate Set" / "Your Everyday Set" value framing rather than pushing single SKUs first — matches your existing merchandising
- **Lifestyle imagery**: product in daily-use context (you already have flower/texture/lifestyle assets in `Products/` — e.g. daylily, hibiscus, peony, scarlet tone shots — these read as premium/botanical positioning and should anchor top-funnel creative)

## Creative Volume Requirements

| Platform | Minimum Active Creatives | Refresh Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Advantage+ Sales | 150+ in campaign | Every 2–4 weeks |
| Meta Standard ad set | 5+ | Every 2–4 weeks |
| Google PMax asset group | Text + 20 images + 5 videos | Every 4–8 weeks |

Given the current asset library (product renders, some lifestyle/texture shots, no video listed), **video is the priority production gap** — especially application/demo video for Bloom and Glow, and unboxing UGC for the Sets.

## Production Priorities (in order)

1. **P1 — Set bundle hero video (15–30s)**: unboxing + "what's inside" for Ultimate Set / Your Everyday Set / Full Glam Set. Meta + Google PMax.
2. **P2 — Static product + lifestyle images with copy overlay**: pull from existing `Products/` renders (All-in-one Set, Color & Glow Set, etc.) reformatted for Meta feed/Stories and Google Shopping (white-bg primary + lifestyle secondary).
3. **P3 — Shade/application demo video**: Catch Bloom lip & cheek swatch/application, Catch Glow texture demo.
4. **P4 — UGC/testimonial video**: real customer routines using a Set — highest-trust format for cold prospecting.
5. **P5 — Claims-locked education creative**: Collagen Shot benefit explainer + Broad Spectrum sunscreen explainer, copy pulled verbatim/adapted from the claims docs, reviewed before launch.

## Seasonal Creative Calendar

- **Q1**: "New year, new routine" — Everyday Set, Glow & Shield Set (pairs skincare + sun protection resolution angle)
- **Q2**: Mother's Day gifting — Full Glam Set, Ultimate Set as gift-ready bundles
- **Q3**: Back-to-school / early fall — Bloom lip & cheek as an everyday-wear angle
- **Q4**: Black Friday/Cyber Monday/holiday — 2–3x creative volume, gift-Set framing, urgency/countdown creative; have this shot and approved by end of September, not November
