# Catch — Budget Plan

## Working Assumption

$8,000/mo (mid-point of your $2k–$20k range). All figures below scale linearly — if actual budget lands at $4k or $15k, apply the same percentages.

## Platform Split

| Platform | % | $/mo @ $8k | $/mo @ $4k | $/mo @ $15k |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta | 65% | $5,200 | $2,600 | $9,750 |
| Google | 35% | $2,800 | $1,400 | $5,250 |

Both clear minimum viable spend at every point in the range (Meta min $600–800/mo, Google min $1,000/mo), so the 65/35 split holds even at $2k/mo — though at the low end, expect a longer learning phase and hold off opening additional asset groups/ad sets until conversion volume supports them.

## 70/20/10 Allocation (apply within each platform once you have 4+ weeks of data)

| Tier | % of platform budget | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Proven | 70% | Best-Seller Sets + Core Lines (Catch Bloom, Catch Glow) — established performers |
| Scaling | 20% | Pairing Sets, new shade launches — showing traction, need more spend to confirm |
| Testing | 10% | Collagen Shot, Sunscreen, new creative formats, new audiences |

Before you have 4 weeks of data, run Month 1 as pure learning (see pacing below) rather than forcing 70/20/10 on day one — there's no "proven" tier yet.

## Monthly Pacing

| Month | Focus | Budget Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Learning | Full budget live, but expect CPA above target. Don't touch budgets or audiences mid-learning-phase. Meta ASC + Google PMax/Search live simultaneously so both platforms accumulate signal in parallel. |
| 3–4 | Optimize | Apply 3× Kill Rule to any ad set/ad group at >3x target CPA with 0 conversions. Tighten Google Search negatives. Begin 70/20/10 split. |
| 5–6 | Scale | Apply 20% Rule to winners (max +20% budget per increase, wait 3–5 days between increases, never on Meta during active learning). Kill anything still underperforming after two optimization cycles. |
| Ongoing | Maintain | Quarterly 70/20/10 rebalance. Re-test Testing-tier winners into Scaling tier. |

## Scaling & Kill Rules (apply exactly as written — don't eyeball it)

- **20% Rule**: actual CPA < target CPA by >10% AND conversions/7d meet learning threshold AND not in active learning phase → increase budget 20%, wait 3–5 days before the next increase.
- **3× Kill Rule**: spend > 3× target CPA with 0 conversions → pause immediately. Don't restart without a change to creative, targeting, or landing page.
- **Decreasing returns**: CPA rises >15% after a budget increase → roll back to the previous budget level, wait 7 days, then try horizontal scaling (new audience/platform) instead of vertical (more $ to the same audience).

## Saturation Watch

| Platform | Signal | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta | 7-day frequency | >4.0 | Refresh creative or expand audience |
| Google | Impression share | >80% | Diminishing returns — diversify into PMax categories or Shopping |

## Seasonal Adjustments (beauty/DTC calendar)

- **Q4 (Oct–Dec)**: increase total budget 40–60% for Black Friday/Cyber Monday and holiday gifting Sets — CPMs rise 30–50% but conversion rate rises too. Front-load creative testing in September so Q4 launches with proven assets, not untested ones.
- **Q1 (Jan–Mar)**: CPMs drop 20–30% post-holiday — best window to test new Set bundles or shade launches at lower acquisition cost.
- **Mother's Day (Q2)**: natural tie-in for gift Sets (Full Glam Set, Ultimate Set) — treat like a mini-Q4.
- **Sale events**: surge budget 20% for 3 days before through the event, not just on the day.

## Source of Truth Hierarchy

Never make a budget decision off platform-reported ROAS alone:
1. CRM/store revenue data (Shopify or equivalent — actual revenue)
2. **MER** = Total Revenue ÷ Total Ad Spend (both platforms combined)
3. Post-purchase survey ("How did you hear about us?") on the order confirmation page
4. GA4 cross-channel view
5. Platform-reported ROAS (Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads) — treat as directional only; typically overclaims 20–40%
